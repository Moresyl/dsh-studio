# Code of Conduct

[简体中文](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.zh-CN.md)

We want DSH Studio to be a useful and safe place for contributors and users. Be respectful, discuss the work rather than the person, assume good faith while accepting correction, and make room for people with different experience and backgrounds.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, doxxing, publishing private credentials, and sustained disruption are not accepted in project spaces. Maintainers may edit or remove content, lock a thread, reject a contribution, or temporarily/permanently restrict participation in proportion to the behavior.

Report ordinary conduct problems privately through [GitHub Security Advisories](https://github.com/Moresyl/dsh-studio/security/advisories/new). Include links or screenshots and the minimum private context needed to investigate. Maintainers will limit disclosure, avoid retaliation, and communicate the outcome when safe to do so.

This policy applies to the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions, release channels, and public representation of the project.
